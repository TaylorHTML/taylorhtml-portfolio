BUDDY

A safety-first companion that helps solo hikers choose a trail, prepare with confidence, navigate offline, and close the loop after the hike.

01The challenge

One hike. Too many disconnected tools.

Solo hikers shouldn’t have to assemble their own safety system.

Planning, trail conditions, navigation, safety check-ins, and gear preparation often live across different apps—or outside an app entirely. That fragmentation creates gaps precisely when clarity matters most.

TrailBuddy connects those moments in one continuous flow: choose a trail, prepare the plan, save the route, navigate without service, and clearly signal a safe return.

DESIGN GOALReduce uncertainty before and during a solo hike.
02Research

Listening before designing

Confidence starts long before the trailhead.

I combined competitive research with interviews about the routines, worries, and workarounds solo hikers rely on in the real world.

01

Preparation creates confidence

Route details, gear, weather, and timing are safety features before the hike even begins.

02

No signal changes everything

Offline navigation and a visible route back matter more than feature depth once service disappears.

03

Safety is shared

Solo hikers still want someone they trust to know the plan, the return time, and when it changes.

04

Clarity beats complexity

On-trail tools have to be fast, legible, and dependable in motion, sunlight, and uncertain conditions.

“I just want someone to know where I am.”— Research participant

04Test + iterate

Observed behavior drove every revision

The prototype became clearer where hesitation showed up.

Two rounds of usability testing exposed the small decisions that could strengthen—or weaken—trust in an outdoor safety tool.

CHANGE_01

Made discovery feel directed

Observed
People needed useful ways to narrow trails without digging through a dense filter system.
Response
Search, nearby conditions, and a small set of visible filters now frame the first decision.
CHANGE_02

Turned preparation into a checkpoint

Observed
A long pre-hike form felt like homework and weakened the feeling of readiness.
Response
Preparation became a short, scannable checklist with one clear next action.
CHANGE_03

Made ending a hike unmistakable

Observed
Participants reached for navigation controls when they meant to finish and save the activity.
Response
A dedicated Trail Controls screen and explicit confirmation separate pausing from ending.
05Final experience

A calmer system for uncertain environments

Prepared before. Supported during. Accounted for after.

USER VALIDATION

Participants described the refined experience as calm, minimal, and easy to navigate. The clearer end-hike flow and focused preparation tools were especially well received.

06Reflection

What I learned

When safety is involved, clarity isn’t polish. It’s the product.

TrailBuddy reinforced the value of connecting research directly to design decisions. Button language, contrast, confirmation, and flow structure all became trust-building tools.

Next, I would test the experience outdoors in real lighting, motion, and connectivity conditions, then explore community trail updates without compromising the focused solo-hiker experience.
